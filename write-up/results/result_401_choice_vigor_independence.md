# Result 401 — Aggregate choice and vigor are independent across subjects

> **⚠️ Exploratory. Numbers from `instructions/memory/hypotheses.md` § H29.**

## Overview

Pooled across all conditions, the proportion of heavy-cookie choices and the mean pressing rate are essentially uncorrelated across subjects: r = −0.018, p = 0.76. The four median-split quadrants (HH, HL, LH, LL) are populated 22–28% each. This is the aggregate null that motivates the threat-resolved choice-vigor analyses in [[result_404]] (which finds the reversal hiding beneath this null) and [[result_402]] (which finds the parameter-mediated cross-channel effects).

## Interpretation

The aggregate independence is consistent with choice and vigor being driven by parameters with orthogonal (or weakly correlated) population distributions: ω drives choice; κ drives vigor; ω and κ are approximately orthogonal in the joint-model fits (see [[result_201]]). When you don't resolve by threat level, the structure cancels out. When you do (as in [[result_404]]), the threat-dependent reversal appears.

The result is methodologically useful: it warns against any analysis that treats "behavioral activation" as a single dimension, and it licenses the four-quadrant framing that the rest of the choice-vigor coupling work uses.
